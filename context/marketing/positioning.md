## One-liner

AI personal stylist in your pocket — snap your outfit, get instant style feedback, and build your digital closet.

## Problem we solve

Getting honest, personalized style advice is hard. Friends are polite, stylists are expensive, and generic fashion content doesn't know *your* wardrobe.

## Solution

GoStylens uses AI to analyze your outfits, suggest improvements, and help you develop a cohesive personal style over time.

## Target audience (ICP)

<!-- Customize -->
- Fashion-conscious adults (22–40) who care about how they look but don't have a personal stylist
- Early adopters comfortable with AI tools
- Mobile-first, iOS/Android

## Differentiation

<!-- Customize — what makes GoStylens different from competitors -->
- Conversational AI stylist (not just "rate my fit")
- Digital closet that learns your wardrobe
- Pose-guided capture for better analysis

## Brand voice

- Confident but not preachy
- Encouraging, never judgmental about body or budget
- Smart friend who knows fashion, not a luxury gatekeeper

## Words we use

- "style analysis", "styling session", "closet", "outfit"
- Avoid: "revolutionary", "disrupt", overly hypey startup speak
