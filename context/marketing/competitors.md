# Competitors / alternatives

Living watchlist for GoStylens competitive intel. Agents research from this list; dated briefs go under `outputs/`. Experiment ideas land in Notion — not here.

**How to use**

1. Keep rows current (links, type, watch focus, last reviewed).
2. Run a competitor scan via the marketing agent → brief → Notion `idea` rows.
3. Prefer public signals only (ASO, LP, social, pricing pages). Label observation vs inference.

**Types**

| Type | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `direct` | AI stylist and/or digital closet overlapping our core loop |
| `adjacent` | Closet, human stylist, or inspiration tools users may substitute |
| `aspirational` | Strong acquisition/brand plays worth studying, not copy |

## Schema (for new rows)

When adding a competitor, fill every column:

- **Name** — product name
- **Type** — `direct` · `adjacent` · `aspirational`
- **Links** — site, App Store, Play, TikTok/IG, Product Hunt (as available)
- **ICP overlap** — High / med / low + one line why
- **Watch focus** — what to research next (ASO, creative, paywall, community, etc.)
- **Last reviewed** — `YYYY-MM-DD` after a brief
- **Notes** — short durable facts only; full research lives in `outputs/`

Keep the list at ~5–12 named products. Archive dead products in Notes rather than deleting history of briefs.
